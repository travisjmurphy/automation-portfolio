## Calgary building-permit sample

`apps/canadian-permit-explorer/public/samples/official-permits.json` is a bounded, field-projected snapshot from the City of Calgary's Building Permits dataset, normalized into the explorer's input format. It was retrieved directly from the official data API, not by executing an Apify Actor.

Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – City of Calgary.

- Dataset: https://data.calgary.ca/Business-and-Economic-Activity/Building-Permits/c2es-76ed
- Licence: https://data.calgary.ca/stories/s/u45n-7awa/
- The exact retrieval timestamp and source query are included in the sample metadata.

The data licence is separate from the code licence. The City of Calgary does not endorse this application. The snapshot is partial and is not a measure of all permits or construction spending in Calgary.
